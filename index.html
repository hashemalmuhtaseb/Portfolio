The free website check

What I look for on every site

These are the problems I see most often on small business websites. Each one costs bookings and calls.

✕ → ✓

The site loads

Expired domains and dead links from Google, Yell or local directories send customers to an error page, or to someone else's site.

✕ → ✓

Works on a phone

Most people search on their phone. Tiny text and sideways scrolling make them give up and pick the next result.

✕ → ✓

Shows the padlock

Without a security certificate, browsers label your site "Not secure", and Google ranks it lower.

✕ → ✓

Hours and prices are clear

Opening times and a price list are the two things customers check before they visit or call.

✕ → ✓

Easy to book or call

A booking button or tap-to-call number on every page, not hidden on a contact page.

✕ → ✓

Up to date

Old copyright years, dead social links and closed platforms like Google+ make a business look closed.

How it works

From first chat to live site

  1. Free check

    I review your current site, or your Google and social listings if you don't have one, and send you a short list of fixes.

  2. Mock-up

    I design your homepage first, free, so you can see it before you commit.

  3. Build

    I build the site with your real menu, prices, photos and hours.

  4. Go live

    I connect your domain, point Google and your directory listings to it, and show you how to update it. It can all be done by email, phone or video call, wherever you are in the UK.
